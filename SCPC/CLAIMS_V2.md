# SCPC v2 claim ledger

## Supported

- Zero false equivalence and complete witnesses on the independent finite and
  random simulation benchmark.
- Positive overall coverage relative to strict exact at matched inputs and
  seeds.
- Scope/conflict ablations demonstrate that both typed guards contribute to the
  observed safety result.

## Not supported

- No official ISO/NIST/CIS equivalence claim.
- No theorem beyond the declared finite relation schema.
- No enterprise deployment claim; this packet is simulation-only by contract.
- No Q1 novelty claim until the literature audit and external adjudication are
  completed in the manuscript stage.
