# SCPC reviewer red-team

- **Novelty:** ontology/crosswalk-only contribution would be rejected; the
  proof/witness object must remain central.
- **Theory:** finite exhaustive checks are not a general proof.
- **Data:** generated labels may encode the classifier; external adjudication
  is mandatory.
- **Experiment:** an external pack with ambiguous scope could reduce coverage
  or expose false witnesses.
- **Release:** Docker, independent host, and standards-text licensing are open.
