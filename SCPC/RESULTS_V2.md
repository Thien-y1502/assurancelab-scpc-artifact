# SCPC v2 independent-holdout results

The v2 run uses five attributes, five seeds, three density regimes and 30,000
paired cases per method. Ground truth is generated by an independent
case-table oracle, not by the production compiler. The finite audit enumerates
4,096 schema rows.

| Method | Accuracy | False-equivalence | Coverage | Scope safety |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SCPC | 1.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.7657 | 1.0000 |
| Strict exact | 0.4611 | 0.0000 | 0.2268 | 1.0000 |
| Ontology rule | 0.6888 | 0.3112 | 0.9166 | 0.0000 |
| Keyword match | 0.4701 | 0.4304 | 0.9166 | 0.0000 |
| Untyped crosswalk | 0.3103 | 0.6897 | 0.9166 | 0.0000 |

The paired across-seed confidence interval for SCPC minus strict-exact
coverage is strictly positive. The independent oracle agrees on all random and
finite rows. Disabling scope or conflict handling creates false equivalences,
supporting the mechanism ablation.

Sparse density lowers SCPC coverage to 0.6684 while preserving zero false
equivalence; this is retained as a robustness boundary rather than hidden.
The released claim is therefore safety-plus-overall-coverage, not uniform
coverage under every density regime.
