# SCPC literature boundary

The closest works are NISTIR 8477 concept mappings, NIS2Onto, ISO-27002
ontology-based compliance, the security-assurance-case SLR, and automated
Petri-net certification of cloud business processes. These establish that
machine-readable mappings and compliance reasoning exist, but also that
assurance-case validation/tool support remains incomplete.

Primary links: [NISTIR 8477](https://www.nist.gov/publications/mapping-relationships-between-documentary-standards-regulations-frameworks-and), [NIS2Onto](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cose.2025.104617), [ISO-27002 ontology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S2056496118000259), [security-assurance-case SLR](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-021-09971-7), [automated cloud-process certification](https://aisel.aisnet.org/bise/vol3/iss3/4/).

The candidate is therefore restricted to a proof-carrying semantic compiler
with explicit relation strength, applicability assumptions, and counterexample
witnesses. It must not claim novelty for ontology construction or a crosswalk
alone. Full citations and URLs are in the parent candidate dossier.
